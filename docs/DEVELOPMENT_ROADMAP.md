# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1 — 3D foundation
- Establish a playable Earth and Mars scene graph.
- Add camera rig and third-person movement.
- Implement collisions, physics, and interaction volumes.

## Phase 2 — Mysha character
- Character model integration.
- Facial and body animation system.
- Outfit switching and animation state logic.

## Phase 3 — Mission flow
- Build the launch, transit, landing, and survival stages.
- Introduce resource tracking and objective logic.

## Phase 4 — Decision systems
- Add branching narrative decisions and ending logic.
- Implement save/load and replay support.

## Phase 5 — polish
- Audio layering, cinematic cuts, and UI polish.
- Data-driven mission tuning and content balancing.

## Phase 6 — expansion
- Additional mission scenarios and deeper scientific integration.
- Replayable content and advanced accessibility features.
