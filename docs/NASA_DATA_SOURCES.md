# NASA Data Sources

The game will eventually integrate official NASA references to improve realism and scientific consistency in the mission design.

## Relevant official sources

- NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 (Human Health and Performance): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/2022-01-05_nasa-std-3001_vol.1_rev._b_final_draft_with_signature_010522.pdf
- NASA ECLSS: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/g-281237_eclss_0.pdf
- NASA MSL RAD Level 2 dataset: https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/mars-science-laboratory-msl-radiation-assessment-detector-rad-level-2-observation-dosimetr-e2695
- NASA MEDA: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/medas-radiation-and-dust-sensor/
- NASA MEDA PDS archive: https://pds-atmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/PERSEVERANCE/meda.html
- JPL Mars 2020 landing press kit: https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/mars_2020/landing/

## Planned usage

- NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1 will guide human health, safety, habitat constraints, and operational limits for crew design decisions.
- NASA ECLSS references will inform oxygen, CO2 management, environmental control, and life-support stress systems.
- NASA MSL RAD Level 2 observations will inform radiation hazard modeling, exposure visuals, and mission risk questions.
- NASA MEDA and the PDS archive will guide dust and atmospheric conditions for Mars survival gameplay.
- JPL Mars 2020 landing materials will support mission timing, landing strategy, and scene realism.

## Reference documents vs observational datasets

- Reference documents: NASA-STD-3001 Volume 1, NASA ECLSS, and JPL landing documentation. These are mission standards and explanatory references.
- Observational datasets: NASA MSL RAD Level 2 and NASA MEDA PDS archive. These provide measured environment and sensor outputs from missions.
- Future use: reference documents set safety and mission-design boundaries; observational datasets add realism to in-game conditions and challenge systems.

## Future distinction between values

- Observed values: measured instrument outputs from actual mission systems.
- Reference values: official NASA ranges, guidelines, or mission design benchmarks.
- Derived values: calculations and composites built from mission data and in-game variables.
- Simulated values: values used to drive balancing, challenge, and narrative decisions within the game world.

No datasets are claimed to be downloaded or integrated yet. These are planned future references for a scientifically grounded mission design.
