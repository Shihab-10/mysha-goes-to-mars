# Game Story Overview

## Cinematic Prologue

A young Mysha grows up fascinated by exploration, science, and the Moon-to-Mars vision. The prologue establishes her personal motivation, commitment to discovery, and the emotional stakes behind the mission.

## Level 1: Launch Preparation

The crew prepares for launch, checks life-support equipment, reviews mission objectives, and confirms the launch schedule. This chapter focuses on readiness, procedure, and careful preparation before leaving Earth.

## Level 2: Space Journey

Mysha travels through deep space with limited resources and increasing technical tension. The mission introduces environmental hazards, emergency repairs, and resource scarcity during the journey to Mars.

## Level 3: Mars Landing

The landing sequence tests crew readiness and system control as the team descends to the Martian surface. Decisions affect safety, landing quality, and the future viability of the mission.

## Level 4: Mars Survival

The crew adapts to the harsh environment, managing habitat systems, dust storms, power systems, and risk. Survival requires careful decisions under stress.

## Final Mission

The final mission resolves the crew's long-term objective and determines whether Mysha can complete the mission and return with the necessary evidence, resources, and narrative outcome.

## Four possible endings

- Mission Success and Return
- Survival with Cost
- Scientific Breakthrough with Sacrifice
- Mission Failure and Loss

The final outcome is shaped by resource management, scenario decisions, and survival performance.
