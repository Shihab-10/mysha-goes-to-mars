# Asset Requirements

## Planned production assets

### Mysha character
- Realistic female astronaut model with clean proportions and believable motion range.
- Multiple outfits for launch, EVA, and habitat use.
- Character animations: idle, walk, run, jump, crouch, reach, interact, and emergency movement.

### Spacecraft and missions
- Launch vehicle and command module models.
- Habitat structures and Mars equipment.
- Mission props: sensors, tools, oxygen tanks, research racks, and emergency kits.

### Environments
- Earth launch environment.
- Deep-space corridor and spacecraft interior.
- Mars landing zone and dusty terrain.
- Habitat and storm event environments.

### Textures and materials
- Mars surface color, roughness, and displacement maps.
- Earth atmospheric textures.
- Spacecraft metal, badge, and interior surfaces.
- Character skin, spacesuit, and trim details.

### Audio
- Cinematic orchestral score.
- Environmental ambience for space and Mars.
- Dialogue cues and mission voice lines.
- SFX for controls, collisions, alarms, storm noise, and tool interaction.

## Actual installed assets

This repository currently contains only the scaffolded project structure and the default Vite app placeholders. No real 3D model files, textures, audio files, or NASA datasets are included in the project yet.
